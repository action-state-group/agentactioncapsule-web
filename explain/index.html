Verifiable records for inference on a mesh of strangers

Sixteen short pages, one per topic. Each page has the same five parts: what it is, the design choices, what it shows and what it does not, one real example with its fields, and the specifications behind it. Each page has one animation of the mechanism; every page opens offline. The last five, from the September deck, add a sixth part: the status of each piece today, with its source.
